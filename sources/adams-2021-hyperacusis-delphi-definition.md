# Adams B, Sereda M, Casey A, Byrom P, Stockdale D, Hoare DJ

A three-round Delphi with 45 hearing-healthcare professionals reached consensus on 42 of 79 statements. It defines hyperacusis as a reduced tolerance to sounds perceived as normal by most people, or by the person before onset. This is the clinician-consensus anchor for hyperacusis as level-driven sound intolerance, distinct from misophonia's trigger-specificity. Abstract-level reading; Tyler et al. 2014's four subtypes (loudness, annoyance, fear, pain) were taken from secondary sources.
