# Jakubovski E, Müller A, Kley H, de Zwaan M, Müller-Vahl K

A household survey of 2,519 German adults using both the Misophonia Questionnaire and the AMISOS-R. 5% met the MQ Severity Scale threshold (single item 1–15, 7 or above clinically significant) and 5.9% met it on AMISOS-R. It describes the MQ's three subscales and the AMISOS-R as a revision of Schröder's 2013 A-MISO-S (10 severity items, 0–4). Clinical misophonia was associated with perfectionism and not-just-right experiences. Full text partly read.
