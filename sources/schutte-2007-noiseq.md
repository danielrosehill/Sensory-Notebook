# Schutte M, Marks A, Wenning E, Griefahn B

The NoiSeQ is a 35-item noise-sensitivity questionnaire derived from the WNSS and the German LEF. It gives a global score plus domain subscales for leisure, work, habitation, communication and sleep. Its overall score correlates highly with the WNSS (r > .83) and has high internal consistency. The domain subscales make it more diagnostic than the WNSS for someone whose problem is domain-specific (home vs work).
