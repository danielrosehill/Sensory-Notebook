# Aazh H

An audiologist's commissioned critique of the 2022 misophonia consensus. It argues the definition does not cleanly separate misophonia from hyperacusis, especially Tyler's 'annoyance hyperacusis', and proposes an exclusion: the primary reaction must not be pain or discomfort from loudness. It argues that environmental noise sensitivity (e.g. neighbours, air conditioning) should be excluded from misophonia. It also calls the 'disorder' label premature and criticises the absence of people with lived experience on the panel.
