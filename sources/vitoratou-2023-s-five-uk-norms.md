# Vitoratou S, Hayes C, Uglik-Marucha N, Pearson O, Graham T, Gregory J

The S-Five has 25 items rated 0–10 across five factors (internalising and externalising appraisals, threat, outbursts, impact); total 0–250. It comes with the S-Five-T trigger checklist (37 triggers). In a UK representative sample (n=772), ROC against clinician interview gave a cut-off of 87 or above for significant misophonia (sensitivity 84%), and estimated prevalence at 18%. The scoring guide is in supplementary table S3, and scoring code is free on request from the first author. Full text searched for the cut-off on 2026-10-14.
