# Greenberg B, Carlos M

The IHS has 25 items rated 1–4 (total up to 100) across five factors: psychosocial impact, emotional arousal, functional impact, general loudness and communication. It was validated on 450 protocols (α = 0.93). The authors proposed 69 as the hyperacusis threshold; a clinical validation by Aazh and colleagues found 56 classified best (79%), so the cut-off is contested. Abstract and poster-level reading.
