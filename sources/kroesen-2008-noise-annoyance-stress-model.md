# Kroesen M, Molin EJE, van Wee B

Models noise annoyance as a psychological stress response, using survey data from residents around Schiphol. Across Kroesen's work, perceived control and mistrust of the responsible authorities emerge as key non-acoustic drivers of annoyance. Cross-sectional survey data cannot settle the direction of causality, and the authors say so. Supports the claim that annoyance is only partly predicted by decibels.
