# Glass DC, Singer JE

Lab experiments found predictable and unpredictable noise rated equally annoying during exposure. Unpredictable noise produced larger after-effects once it stopped: more proofreading errors and lower frustration tolerance. Soft unpredictable noise produced slightly more errors than loud predictable noise. Believing one could stop the noise largely abolished the after-effects even when the option was never used, and a replication linked lack of control to raised catecholamines.
