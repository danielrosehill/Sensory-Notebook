# Jager I, de Koning P, Bost T, Denys D, Vulink N

Of 779 people referred to the Amsterdam UMC misophonia clinic (2013–2017), 575 (74%) were confirmed. Comorbid ADHD was 5% (31/575), against 26% obsessive-compulsive personality traits and 10% mood disorders; hyperacusis was reported by 1% and tinnitus by 2%. Audiometry in a subgroup was largely normal. The study proposes revised diagnostic criteria; the authors note single-site selection bias. Abstract-level reading.
