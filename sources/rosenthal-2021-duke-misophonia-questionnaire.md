# Rosenthal MZ, et al

The DMQ is an 86-item modular measure covering trigger checklist and frequency, affective, physiological and cognitive responses, coping, impairment and beliefs. It was the first English-language misophonia measure validated with factor analysis plus IRT. The 23-item Symptom Scale is scored 0–4; no clinical cut-off is published. It is distributed free as a fillable PDF by the Duke Center for Misophonia and Emotion Regulation (https://psychiatry.duke.edu/sites/default/files/2023-11/Duke%20Misophonia%20Questionnaire_v1.3_Fillable.pdf). The Duke site and the paper disagree slightly on two subscale item counts.
