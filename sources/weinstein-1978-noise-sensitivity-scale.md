# Weinstein ND

This is the origin of the Weinstein Noise Sensitivity Scale (WNSS), 21 items rated 0–5 with 7 reverse-keyed. It treats noise sensitivity as a stable individual trait on a continuum, with no clinical cut-off. It remains the most widely used noise-sensitivity measure, and its psychometrics were better evaluated than most early instruments (Zimmer & Ellermeier 1999).
