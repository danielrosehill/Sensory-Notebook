# Siepsiak M, Śliwerski A, Dragan WŁ

A 14-item questionnaire rated 1–5 (total 14–70), developed from Schröder's criteria in an online sample of 705, with α = 0.955 and strong five-week stability. A score of 61 or above flags probable misophonia, set as the misophonia-group mean minus one SD and supported by later ROC analysis. Items are reproduced in Table 1 of the open-access paper. Later validated in Brazil (α = 0.93).
