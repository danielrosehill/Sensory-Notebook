# Khalfa S, Dubal S, Veuillet E, Perez-Diaz F, Jouvent R, Collet L

Normalised in 201 general-population participants, the 14-item questionnaire separates into attentional, social and emotional dimensions. Secondary sources cite 28 as the high-sensitivity threshold. Later clinical validations proposed lower cut-offs: 16 in Italy (sensitivity 67.9%, specificity 72.2%) and 14 for the revised Turkish version. Descriptions of the response scale disagree (0–3 vs 0–4). The original paper was not read, so verify the cut-off and scale before relying on them.
