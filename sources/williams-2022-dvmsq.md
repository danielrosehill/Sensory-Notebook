# Williams ZJ, Cascio CJ, Woynaroski TG

The DVMSQ is a brief screener: 17 scored items (10 symptom, 7 impairment, 0–4; total 0–68). Rather than a cut-off, it classifies caseness with a criterion-based algorithm (A–F), giving 'clinically significant' vs 'sub-clinical' misophonia.
