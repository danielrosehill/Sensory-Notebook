# Noise is beneficial for cognitive performance in ADHD

Included because it is the source of the apparent paradox — white noise **helps** ADHD
while background speech **hurts** — and because resolving that paradox is most of the
answer to why speech specifically is the trigger.

## The Moderate Brain Arousal (MBA) model

From Sikström & Söderlund (2007), *Psychological Review*. Environmental noise enters the
neural system through perception and induces **stochastic resonance**: in any system
where signal plus noise must cross a threshold (e.g. the all-or-none action potential), a
weak signal can be reinforced by added random noise. Too little noise and the signal does
not cross; too much and it is swamped. There is an optimum.

The model's specific prediction: **the amount of noise needed for the optimum is
modulated by dopamine level.** Lower-dopamine individuals need more external noise to
reach the peak. Hence the signature cross-over interaction — the same acoustic stimulation
that helps an ADHD group hurts a control group.

## Findings

Children with ADHD and controls performed a high-memory task (self-performed mini tasks)
and a low-memory verbal task, with and without auditory white noise. Noise **improved**
performance in the ADHD group and **impaired** it in controls.

## Why this does not contradict the speech findings

White noise is **stationary and unstructured**. It carries no changing-state order cues
and no phonology, so it does not engage the mechanisms that produce the irrelevant sound
effect — it just raises the arousal floor. Speech is **non-stationary and structured**,
and that structure is precisely what does the damage (see
[`leist-2025-irrelevant-speech-verbal-vs-spatial.md`](leist-2025-irrelevant-speech-verbal-vs-spatial.md)
and [`hongisto-haapakangas-sti-performance.md`](hongisto-haapakangas-sti-performance.md)).

So "noise helps me, people talking destroys me" is not a contradiction and not idiosyncratic.
The two literatures predict it jointly.

## Status of the evidence — read this before relying on it

This is the shakiest source in the set.

- Replications are mixed. Positive effects have been reported for children with elevated
  inattention traits on verbal and visuospatial working memory and on Go/NoGo tasks
  (Baijot et al. 2016; Helps et al. 2014), with the cross-over in typically developing
  peers. But white noise did **not** affect verbal 2-back performance in children with
  ADHD (Söderlund et al. 2016).
- The mechanism is under direct attack: "Stochastic resonance is not required for pink
  noise to have beneficial effects on ADHD-related performance? The moderate brain
  arousal model challenged," *Neuropsychologia*, 2024
  (doi:10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2024.108932 — DOI **not verified**, see provenance).
- The dopamine-modulation claim is a model assumption, not something these experiments
  measured.
- Pink noise is often substituted for white because it is less aversive to participants.

**The useful, low-risk reading:** the *contrast* between structured speech and
unstructured broadband noise is well supported by several independent literatures. The
*dopaminergic stochastic-resonance explanation* for why noise helps is contested. You can
lean on the first without buying the second.
