# Bar-Shalita T, Seltzer Z, Vatine JJ, Yochman A, Parush S

An Israeli-developed, 58-item self-report measure of sensory modulation for ages 14+, covering everyday situations across non-pain modalities. It scores an Aversive subscale (32 items, indexing sensory over-responsivity) and a Hedonic subscale (26 items). Internal consistency was .90–.93 and test–retest .71–.84. In a misophonia study, misophonia participants scored higher on both subscales without being classed as the SOR type.
