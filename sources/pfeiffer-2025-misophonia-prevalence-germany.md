# Pfeiffer E, Allroggen M, Sachser C

A representative German sample (n≈2,522): 33.3% reported sensitivity to at least one misophonic sound, while moderate-to-severe symptoms were about 2%. The authors describe typical triggers as sounds made directly or indirectly by other people, repeatedly occurring and usually not excessively loud. Useful as population-level support for the 'not loudness-driven' characterisation. Abstract-level reading.
