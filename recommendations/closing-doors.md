# Closing doors

Personal experience, not evidence. Not medical advice — see the disclaimer in the
[README](../README.md).

## The problem

Someone else's television, audible from where I am trying to study or work. This started
in childhood and it has not gone away — it is still my situation today when it happens.

The specific case is a household one: other people are doing something entirely reasonable
in their own space, and it lands in mine.

## What was tried

Close the door.

That is the whole intervention, and it is worth writing down precisely because it is the
one nobody writes down. It is free, it takes two seconds, it requires no purchase and no
appointment, and it is the first thing I reach for before anything else on this list.

## What happened

It works, often enough to be the default. Where it works it is better than every other
option here, because there is nothing in your ears.

## What it did not fix

- **It fails on volume.** If it is loud enough, a closed door is not enough, and I escalate
  to earplugs — [`custom-moulded-earplugs.md`](custom-moulded-earplugs.md).
- **It only works where you control the door.** It is useless in an open-plan office, which
  is the single worst environment on record in [`../context/profile.md`](../context/profile.md),
  and useless anywhere the sound and you are in the same room.
- **It is a social act, not just an acoustic one.** You are closing a door on people. In a
  shared home that carries a meaning beyond the decibels, and the cost of that is real even
  when it is small. This is the part that makes it harder than it sounds.

## Transferability

- **Try this before buying anything.** The cheapest intervention in this notebook, and the
  one most likely to be skipped over on the way to a product.
- **It generalises to any door you control** — but note that the reason it works is
  probably not only attenuation. A door also removes the sightline and the sense of the
  activity happening at you. Which of those is doing the work here is not established.
  <!-- FLAG: speculation. Do not let this harden into a mechanism claim. -->
- **Treat it as step one of a ladder, not a solution on its own.** The full escalation in
  practice is: close the door → earplugs → (earmuffs over the top, tried and not adopted,
  see [`earmuffs.md`](earmuffs.md)).
