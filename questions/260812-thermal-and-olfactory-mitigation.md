# What's the equivalent of earplugs for heat and for smell?

"Medication" is the wrong word for what I mean — mitigation.

For the auditory side we have earplugs and earmuffs, and I know where I stand with them.
But I've also mentioned that I sometimes struggle with overheating, and with smell as a
sensory distractor. For those, it's much less obvious what the equivalent is.

We should look at those aspects as well, to begin to shape up a more rounded picture.
