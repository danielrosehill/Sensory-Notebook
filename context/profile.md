## Per modality

For each: what the trigger is, how it presents, how severe, what makes it worse or better.

### Auditory

**Primary trigger: intelligible background speech, while I am trying to concentrate.**

- Background conversation is the problem — people talking nearby, someone on a phone
  call in the same room. Overheard *speech* specifically, not noise in general and not
  loudness.
- It is **state-dependent, and that is the defining feature.** If I am relaxing in the
  same room, with the same people having the same conversations at the same volume, it
  does not bother me at all. It only becomes a problem when I am trying to focus on
  something.
- I cannot tune it out. It is not that I notice it and then let it go; the filtering
  step other people appear to have does not happen for me.
- Open-plan offices are inherently very challenging for this reason.
- My hearing itself is normal. I have had hearing testing done and it came back
  completely normal — see [`story.md`](story.md).
